Our Mission

Eradicating Silicosis Misdiagnosis in Rajasthan.

We are a dedicated MedTech startup answering the call of the Rajasthan State Compensation Policy to protect the thousands of sandstone mine workers suffering in silence.

The Challenge

Silicosis prevalence among Rajasthan's sandstone mine workers runs as high as 79% in districts like Karauli, Jodhpur, and Bhilwara. Tragically, this irreversible lung disease is routinely misdiagnosed as tuberculosis because early-stage silicosis shows no symptoms until the lung damage is fatal.

Workers in these remote mining belts rarely have access to hospital-grade chest X-rays or spirometry facilities. By the time they receive a proper diagnosis, the damage is irreversible.

Our Core Philosophy

Bring the Hospital to the Desert

We do not believe in forcing dying miners to travel 100 miles to a district hospital for screening. MaruCure is designed to place hospital-grade radiological intelligence and physical lung capacity math directly into the hands of local Asha workers on cheap $150 tablets.

The Double-Pillar Paradigm

A single "triage score" is clinically dangerous. A miner might have severe lung nodules but breathe perfectly for one test. We explicitly separated MaruCure into two unblended hazard pillars: Anatomical (X-Ray) and Physiological (Spirometry). If a miner triggers a critical flag on either pillar, our system instantly demands secondary medical care.

Zero Reliance on Infrastructure

Sandstone quarries do not have 4G cell service. A cloud-based AI system will fail on day one. We built MaruCure using WebAssembly and IndexedDB to guarantee 100% offline functionality. The math runs locally, safely queuing data until the worker returns to civilization.

Seamless Government Integration

Identifying a sick worker is only half the battle. They need compensation and care. MaruCure securely synchronizes with the Rajasthan Jan Aadhaar system via mTLS, automatically referring high-risk miners directly to the Pneumoconiosis Board.
